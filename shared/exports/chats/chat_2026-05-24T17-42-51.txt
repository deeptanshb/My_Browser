AI:  To swap two numbers in Python, you can use the following simple method:

```python
a = 5
b = 10

# Swapping a and b
a, b = b, a

print(a) # Outputs: 10
print(b) # Outputs: 5
```

In this code, `a` and `b` are the two numbers you want to swap. The line with `a, b = b, a` swaps the values of `a` and `b`.
